Grid

Snapping moves a cutout's or text's centre onto grid points (also sheet corners and measurements). Hold Alt while dragging to move freely. With snap on, arrow keys move one grid step.

Finger grips

Select a cutout and drag its grip handle: edge grips (yellow) slide along the edge, centre grips (orange) move freely. A centre grip only cuts where it's wider than the tool. Alt+click a handle to remove it.

Text

Text is engraved (blue layer). Drag it on this tab to move it; double-click it to edit.
